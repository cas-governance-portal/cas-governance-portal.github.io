    Alignment Findings

    • [MEDIUM] 22 proposed items without progression.
    • Recommendation:
    • [HIGH] The AID is mostly empty: strategic vision is generic, Why it exists is blank, and current state alignment, focus areas, gaps, principles, and emergent heuristics are empty. This is inconsistent with the AEM, which contains detailed system evolution, intent direction, pattern evolution, and knowledge-system health content.
    • Recommendation: Rebuild or validate the AID from accepted ADRs, AEM, and Architecture Work. Populate strategic vision, principles, focus areas, risks, current-state alignment, and emergent heuristics using governed sources only.
    • [HIGH] The Architecture Work registry contains a dummy_string record and a discovery work item whose title and content are dummy_string. The AEM summary counts one dummy_string item, and the AID aw_count of 30 includes these placeholder records.
    • Recommendation: Remove or quarantine the dummy_string record, repair aw-discovery-ideas-to-life-cas-1 with real discovery evidence or mark it superseded, then regenerate AEM and AID counts from the cleaned Architecture Work registry.
    • [HIGH] AEM evolution map entries and today's actions use READY, PENDING, APPROVED, and BLOCKED tags, while the underlying Architecture Work items are mostly proposed or done. Several items marked READY in the AEM correspond to AW records with status proposed, which introduces planning state in a generated artifact.
    • Recommendation: Make AEM stage and readiness fully derived from AW status, decision status, forum status, and explicit blocker conditions. Add validation that prevents the AEM from introducing new planning states not present in Architecture Work.
    • [HIGH] Some decision-bearing records show non-accepted decision status while being treated as accepted, done, or promoted. aw-ideas-to-life-cas-2026-05-31-001 is status done and mapped to AEM DONE, while its detail says Decision Status proposed. The pattern evolution notes also state that heuristic-driven-arbitration has accepted forum status but a proposed decision record, and sequential-pipeline-orchestration has a placeholder date with low evidence strength.
    • Recommendation: Synchronize decision.status with forum_status where the architect has accepted the decision, update AW lifecycle states accordingly, correct placeholder dates, and re-derive AEM stages from validated decision evidence.
    • [MEDIUM] The AEM contains conflicting or ambiguous pattern states: pattern-classification-framework is emerging with pending governance, while pattern-scope-classification-framework is promoted and accepted. Several emerging patterns are labelled core before governance acceptance, and a rejected pattern remains classified as core.
    • Recommendation: Determine whether pattern-classification-framework and pattern-scope-classification-framework are the same concept. If duplicate, supersede one and update references. If distinct, ensure only governance-accepted patterns are promoted and demote pending or conditional emerging patterns to supporting until promotion evidence is satisfied.
    • [MEDIUM] sequential-pipeline-orchestration is promoted as an accepted core pattern, but the AEM notes that evidence strength is low and the date field contains a placeholder. This reduces confidence that the promotion satisfies evidence-based governance requirements.
    • Recommendation: Correct the decision date, add multi-source evidence for sequential-pipeline-orchestration, or downgrade the pattern to supporting or pending until promotion evidence is sufficient.
    • [MEDIUM] The AID references aem-ideas-to-life-cas-2026-10-07-1, but the provided AEM frontmatter id is aem-ideas-to-life-cas-2026-10-07. The AID also reports alignment_findings: 0, while this report identifies concrete findings. The AEM evolution map does not explicitly trace every one of the 30 Architecture Work items, leaving some items implicit.
    • Recommendation: Correct the AID source references, generate or update the AID after alignment findings are ingested, and add explicit AEM-to-AW traceability or documented aggregation notes for all 30 Architecture Work items.
    • [MEDIUM] The AEM reports signal_pattern_linkage as medium, while many promoted patterns are described as accepted through synchronized ArchGov review without visible linked signal evidence. Heuristic-derived patterns and promoted core patterns should retain traceable signal evidence and promotion criteria.
    • Recommendation: Annotate each heuristic or promoted core pattern with at least one linked signal, recurrence or impact evidence, and a promotion decision reference. Downgrade patterns that cannot demonstrate sufficient evidence.
