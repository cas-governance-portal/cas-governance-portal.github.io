    System Evolution

    CAS has moved from a decision-driven architecture documentation system toward a continuous architecture governance platform. Completed work established the Architecture Work Registry as the authoritative architectural backlog, codified the CAS Workflow Execution Model, introduced the Knowledge Provider abstraction, converted prompt-native workflows into runtime-native capabilities, and published a Governance Portal. The governing substrate now relies on deterministic generator contracts, a runtime capability inventory, target-project evidence isolation, and forensic architecture-state diagnostics. Accepted ADRs promote provider-agnostic LLM facade/gateway/client patterns, sequential pipeline orchestration, file-backed blackboard workspaces, finite-state governance, schema-enforced domain contracts, telemetry-observable agent steps, and rule-grounded compliance. The next system evolution is proposed rather than governance-accepted: continuous architecture governance runtime, automated detect-analyse-execute loops, architecture intelligence layer, portfolio intelligence, and architecture knowledge graph.

    Intent & Direction

    Project-scoped intent points toward an Architecture Intelligence Platform connecting business intent, architecture, code, and decisions. Vision-derived Architecture Work proposes business intent integration and traceability, code intelligence, architecture alignment, ReasoningOps and GovernanceOps operational loops, external ecosystem ingestion, interactive Architecture Intelligence Maps, and a knowledge graph. These are NEXT-stage proposals, not system-accepted commitments until ADR governance accepts them. The direction remains anchored in extending existing CAS primitives, keeping decisions central, preferring traceability over abstraction, and preserving human governance as authoritative.
