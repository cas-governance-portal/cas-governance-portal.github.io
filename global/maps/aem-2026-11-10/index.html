    System Evolution

    The system has evolved from a manual documentation and governance model into a Continuous Architecture Governance platform. The Architecture Work Registry is now the authoritative backlog, the CAS Workflow Execution Model is established, and the Knowledge Provider abstraction decouples workflows from repository structure. Governance artifacts (portal, alignment frontmatter) have been made operational. The system is moving toward automated governance loops (Detect-Analyse-Execute), runtime capabilities, and intelligence layers that connect business intent, code, and architecture. The focus is on closing the loop between intent, architecture, implementation, and governance, with lightweight governance participation embedded in delivery workflows.

    Intent & Direction

    The system is heading toward an Architecture Intelligence Platform that connects business intent with architecture and code. Key directions include: (1) business intent integration and traceability from BRDs to decisions; (2) code intelligence for structural analysis and deviation detection; (3) continuous alignment between AID (intent) and implementation; (4) operational intelligence loops for ReasoningOps and GovernanceOps; (5) external ecosystem integration via plugin/ingestion framework; (6) interactive Architecture Intelligence Maps and knowledge graph for exploration. The evolution path is guided by principles: extend don't rewrite, keep decisions central, prefer traceability over abstraction, human governance remains authoritative, and start from real use cases. Phase 3 will focus on business intent traceability, enterprise integration, multi-system intelligence, and selective governance automation.
