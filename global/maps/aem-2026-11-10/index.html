    System Evolution

    The system has moved from a decision-driven architecture system to a continuous governance platform. Key evolutions include: establishment of the Architecture Work Registry as the authoritative architectural backlog; introduction of the Knowledge Provider abstraction to decouple workflows from repository structure; standardization of the CAS Workflow Execution Model (Runner, Workflow, Knowledge Provider, Prompt contracts); implementation of the Governance Portal and alignment summary frontmatter; and adoption of multiple core patterns around governance (continuous governance loop, finite-state governance engine, registry-guarded state machines) and intelligence (runtime capability inventory, forensic state diagnostics). Automated and semi-automated detection loops are being proposed, shifting governance from periodic review towards continuous participation. The architecture work registry is now the authoritative planning source, and all generated artifacts are derived projections rather than independent planning state.

    Intent & Direction

    The forward vision moves CAS from architecture extraction to an Architecture Intelligence Platform. Key intents include: business intent integration and traceability, code intelligence and architecture alignment, operational intelligence loops for ReasoningOps and GovernanceOps, external ecosystem ingestion, interactive architecture intelligence maps, and a knowledge-graph layer. These are project-scoped directions and should not be generalized globally. The immediate roadmap prioritizes building ADRs for core orchestration pipeline and business intent integration, followed by CI validation and continuous governance automation. Long-term capabilities such as portfolio intelligence and architecture intelligence maps remain future-oriented and are not yet active commitments.
