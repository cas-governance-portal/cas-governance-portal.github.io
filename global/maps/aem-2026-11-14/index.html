    The supplied project backlog proposes three foundation changes: deterministic runtime capability visibility, project-local decision capture, and clearer intake/classification/synthesis boundaries. All remain proposed and therefore NEXT; no completed implementation, introduced workflow, or measured operational improvement is evidenced.     Intent & Direction

    For ea4all-automation, the vision is a lightweight, composable, evidence-led architecture practice that automates repeatable execution while preserving human judgement and accountability. The proposed inventory, local ADR capture, and component-boundary work align with that direction. Narrow experiments in evidence intake, consistency checking, structured assessment preparation, and traceable report assembly remain design hypotheses, not additional committed backlog. Provenance, explicit uncertainty, structured intermediate artifacts, and consequential human review should guide evaluation. Benefits require real delivery evidence; time savings, evidence completeness, rework, traceability, stakeholder usefulness, and reuse are suggested measures, not observed results.
